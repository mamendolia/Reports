# Changelog

## knowbe4-training-report-v1.0.0 – 2026-11-18

First public release.

- Offline single-file tool for the KnowBe4 detailed training export (CSV/XLSX), network blocked by Content Security Policy.
- Completion per tenant and group total: completion, full completion, users in good standing, management bodies, PPP, distinct courses and courses per user.
- Campaign search and selection, assignment-period filter, cross-campaign de-duplication.
- Management bodies from KnowBe4 groups (search, suggestions) and e-mail list.
- Group-wide and per-tenant top performers with a documented scoring rule.
- Delivery plan vs. export, with Italian↔English title matching and manual pairing saved in the browser.
- Formatted Excel report (dashboard, plan, management, rankings, users, audit register, data-quality notes).
- Data-quality section listing every interpretation made on the export.
